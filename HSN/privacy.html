Hide&Seek Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Hide&Seek is a private photo and video gallery. It lets you keep photos and videos in folders, protected by a passcode, and open them from any device by signing in with your Google account.

What we collect

Hide&Seek has no servers and no database of its own. We do not collect, receive, or store your photos, videos, passcode, or recovery code. Everything runs in your browser.

What is stored, and where

  • Your photos and videos are encrypted in your browser before they are saved. They are stored in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive.
  • Your folder list and settings are stored in the same hidden app folder. This includes your encryption key, which is itself locked by your passcode and by your recovery code.
  • Your passcode and recovery code are never sent anywhere, and we cannot see them.

Google permission we ask for

Hide&Seek asks for two things when you sign in: access to its own app data folder in Google Drive (drive.appdata), and your basic Google profile (name, email address, and profile picture) so the app can show who is signed in. It cannot see, read, or change any of your other Drive files, and it does not read your Gmail, contacts, or calendar. Your name, email, and picture are kept only in your own browser on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, share, or give your data to anyone.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking.
  • We do not read or look at your photos or videos.

Your passcode and recovery code

Because we do not hold your data, we cannot recover it. If you forget your passcode, the recovery code is the only way to reset it. If you lose both, your photos cannot be opened by anyone, including us.

Deleting your data

You can delete your data at any time. In your Google Account, open Data & privacy, then Third-party apps and services, choose Hide&Seek, and remove its access and its stored app data. Removing that data permanently deletes your photos, videos, and folders from Hide&Seek.

Children

Hide&Seek is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will be posted on this page.
